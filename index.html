OneRemote Privacy Policy

Developer: Ibrahim Salah, creator of OneRemote

OneRemote is provided by Ibrahim Salah as an individual developer.

What OneRemote does

OneRemote controls compatible TVs over your local network. You do not need a OneRemote account. Remote commands are sent directly to the selected TV.

Information stored on your device

OneRemote stores TV addresses, names, brands, room labels, favorite controls, favorite channel names and numbers, connection dates, and pairing information. These support reconnection and your personalized TV library. Some pairing keys use the system Keychain; others use local app preferences.

Removing a TV removes its saved library entry, but does not necessarily remove pairing credentials stored separately. Keychain items may remain after uninstalling the app.

Usage and crash information

OneRemote uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand feature usage, troubleshoot failures, and improve compatibility and stability. These services can collect installation identifiers, device and operating-system details, app information, usage events, and crash reports.

OneRemote's custom events include TV brand, connection events and errors, and the names of TV apps launched through OneRemote. Network errors may contain local-network information. Providers receive technical network information, such as IP addresses, when the app communicates with their services.

See Firebase's privacy information for more information about Google's processing.

Purchases and developer notifications

Apple processes payments. OneRemote reads Apple-verified transaction information to determine Pro access and restore purchases. OneRemote does not receive your payment-card details.

For verified production purchases, OneRemote sends the product identifier and displayed price through EmailJS for a notification to the developer. The app does not include the purchaser's name or email address in these notifications. EmailJS and the connected email service process the request and its technical metadata. Xcode and sandbox purchases do not send these notifications.

Support messages

If you email the support address, the developer receives your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide. These are used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Please do not send passwords or TV pairing codes.

Service providers and international processing

Apple, Google/Firebase, EmailJS, and the connected email service process information for the purposes described above. Their infrastructure may process information outside your country. Their own privacy policies and service terms also govern their handling of information.

See Apple's privacy policy, Google's privacy policy, and EmailJS's privacy policy.

Retention

Local TV settings remain until removed or app data is cleared. Pairing information is stored separately and may persist as described above.

The OneRemote Google Analytics property is configured to retain event data for 2 months and user data for 14 months. User-data retention resets on new user activity. These settings do not apply to standard aggregated reports. Google removes data according to its retention processing schedule; the periods are not a promise that all data disappears immediately on a particular day. No BigQuery export is configured for this Analytics property.

Firebase Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems.

The EmailJS purchase-notification template saves email history. The account's Free plan retains email history for 7 days. EmailJS may retain other service or abuse-prevention records under its own privacy policy. Delivered notification emails and support correspondence remain in the developer's mailbox until deleted; deleting EmailJS history does not delete delivered email copies.

See Google Analytics retention, Firebase retention information, and EmailJS retention by plan.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can edit or remove saved TVs in My TVs, revoke local-network permission in iPhone Settings, and manage subscription renewal through your Apple Account. Buying Lifetime Pro does not cancel a separate subscription.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise control personal information. Email oneremote.support@gmail.com to ask about data handling or make a privacy request. Describe the request and relevant app use; do not include passwords or payment-card details.

The developer may need additional information to verify a request and identify relevant records. OneRemote has no user accounts, so the developer may be unable to associate installation-based analytics or crash records with your identity from an email address alone. Requests are handled subject to applicable law, available identifying information, and any required retention obligations.
